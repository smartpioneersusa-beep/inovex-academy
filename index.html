Frequently asked questions

Who are INOVEX Academy courses for?

For everyone who works on a coating line: operators, technicians, maintenance and quality staff, engineers, supervisors and sales engineers in powder coating, liquid painting and enamel plants.

Where is the training delivered?

At INOVEX Academy in Riyadh or at your factory anywhere in Saudi Arabia and the GCC — theory, hands-on practice on real equipment, or both.

In which languages?

Classroom and on-site training in Arabic or English. The online course book, presentation and assessment are available in English, Arabic, Urdu and Hindi.

How do trainees get access to the online courses?

Online courses are included with INOVEX training packages. Each company receives access codes; every code opens 30 days of access on this portal.

Do trainees receive a certificate?

Yes. Completion, attendance or qualification certificates are issued by INOVEX Academy, authorised by Wagner Group and in association with Al Qemma Training Institute. Every certificate has an ID that anyone can verify online.
